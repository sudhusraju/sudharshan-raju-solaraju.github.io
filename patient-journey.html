Problem Statement

The Challenge Facing Hospital Operations

Hospitals manage thousands of patient interactions daily across multiple departments, wards, clinics, and care pathways. Without a unified view, critical operational and clinical data remained locked in siloed systems — making it nearly impossible for leadership to spot trends, manage capacity, or improve patient outcomes in real time.

📊 Fragmented Data Silos

Patient visit data, admission records, discharge summaries, and medication logs existed in separate systems with no single consolidated view for decision-makers.

⏱️ No Real-Time Visibility

Hospital administrators had no live tracking of ER congestion, ward occupancy, or admission rates — leading to reactive rather than proactive capacity management.

🏥 Unknown Patient Flow Patterns

The full patient journey from first visit → diagnosis → admission → discharge → medication was invisible, making it impossible to identify bottlenecks or inefficiencies.

💊 Medication Tracking Gaps

No clear picture of which medications were being distributed at scale, hindering pharmacy planning, procurement, and clinical protocol reviews.

02
Solution

An End-to-End Patient Intelligence Platform

I designed and built a fully interactive Power BI dashboard that unifies every stage of the patient journey into a single, filterable, real-time report — empowering clinical leads and hospital administrators with actionable intelligence at a glance.

"Every patient touchpoint — from the moment they walk in to the medication they leave with — is now visible in one place, updated daily."

— Dashboard Design Philosophy

🔀 Patient Journey Flow Visual

A funnel-style flow chart mapping Visits → Diagnosed → Admissions → Discharged → Medication, giving leadership an instant bird's-eye view of patient throughput.

📈 Visit & Admission Trend Analysis

Dual time-series line chart tracking total visits vs. admissions over months — enabling seasonal pattern recognition and forward capacity planning.

🏨 Ward-Level Admissions Breakdown

Comparative bar chart across Ward A, B, C, ICU, and CCU — helping operations teams allocate staffing and beds efficiently based on real demand.

🩺 Diagnosis by Visit Type

Ranked horizontal bar showing top diagnoses (Type 2 Diabetes, Hypertension, Pneumonia, etc.) — critical for clinical teams to align resources and protocols.

💊 Medicine Distribution Tracker

Top 10 medications distributed ranked by volume (Ceftriaxone, Paracetamol, Omeprazole, etc.) — directly supporting pharmacy inventory and procurement decisions.

🎯 Dynamic Slicers & Filters

7 interactive slicers — Region, Clinic, Visit Type, Visit Date, Nationality, Gender, Age Group — enabling personalised drill-down for any user across any segment.

❤️ Patient Health Outcome Analysis

Donut chart visualising patient outcomes: Recovered, Stable, Follow-up Required, Improved — giving clinical teams a quality-of-care performance metric.

📉 Admission Rate % Over Time

Volatility chart showing admission rate fluctuation from March to November 2025, revealing spikes and dips that signal seasonal illness patterns or operational issues.
